# Agricultural farm-diesel regional screen — sources and boundaries

2021 census regional district data: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/agriculture-seafood/statistics/census-of-agriculture . Eight district Agriculture-in-Brief profiles are linked individually in workbook and CSV. These are official counts/land uses, NOT fuel volumes. For forage-field work reference: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11367-023-02199-1 (published cross-Canada estimates ~22–36 L/ha of perennial forage). Remaining machinery coefficients are screening assumptions only. Diesel 2026 combustion factor 2,681 g CO2/L: https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/climate-change/pricing-pollution-how-it-will-work/output-based-pricing-system/federal-greenhouse-gas-offset-system/emission-factors-reference-values.html . B.C. fuel-tax exemptions: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/sales-taxes/motor-fuel-carbon-tax/business/exemptions . 

Eight entire regional districts are substantially wider than 17 municipal boundaries. Municipal-level parcel/field data, measured tractor/hour diesel meters, farm road trips, irrigation diesel, farm-fuel purchase invoices, fuel tax class, renewable-fuel quotes and delivery agreements are needed for a full audited municipal coverage claim. Farm diesel model includes a generic assumed farm-service allowance but excludes fuel for forestry, aquaculture vessels, off-farm trucking and centralized food distribution. Original 100 million L is an unverified assumption, retained only as comparative cap, NOT a census fact. Site attribution weights are assumptions and do not identify actual farms within city limits. The 2021 census counts are not a 2026 farm inventory. Fuel rates and pricing are scenarios and should not be described as actual expenditures.
